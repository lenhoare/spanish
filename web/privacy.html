Privacy notice

Last updated: [DATE]

This notice explains what information the Ms Cleary website (mscleary.com) and its games use, why, and what your choices are. It's written to be read by teachers, parents and students.

The short version
  • Students don't have accounts. We never ask for a student's name, email or any other personal details.
  • Game progress (stars, sweets, challenges) is saved only on the device you play on. It isn't sent to us.
  • Teachers who sign in give us their name and email address, from Google or Microsoft, so we can run their account and classes.
  • Card payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store card details.
  • We don't sell data, we don't show adverts, and we don't use tracking or advertising cookies.

Playing the games (everyone)

Progress saved on your device

The game remembers your best star rating, sweets found and challenges completed on each island, so you can carry on next time. This is stored in your web browser on your own device. It is not sent to us, and we can't see it. Clearing your browser's site data removes it.

Information needed to deliver the website

Like every website, when your browser asks for a page, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) receives technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested. This is used to deliver the site, keep it secure (for example, against attacks), and fix problems. Our own error logs that include this information are kept for a few days and then deleted automatically.

Teacher accounts

Teachers can sign in to set up classes. You choose to sign in with Google or Microsoft (for example, your school Microsoft 365 / Teams account). We receive and keep:

  • your name and email address;
  • an account identifier from Google or Microsoft, so we recognise you next time;
  • when you last signed in;
  • the classes you create: the class name you type and its join code.

We don't receive your Google or Microsoft password, and we don't get access to your email, files, contacts or calendar.

Please don't put students' names in class names. Use something like "9X Spanish".

Students joining a class

When a class code is required, a student types the code their teacher gave them (or opens the teacher's link). The site then stores a small cookie on that device saying which class it has joined, so the student can play. It contains no name or personal details, and we don't record who the student is.

Subscriptions and payments

Teacher subscriptions are paid through Stripe. You enter your card details on Stripe's own secure page. They never reach us. Stripe tells us your customer reference and whether your subscription is active, trialling, overdue or cancelled, and we keep that so the right classes work. Stripe uses your information as described in Stripe's privacy notice, including to prevent fraud.

Cookies

We only use cookies that are needed for the site to work, so we don't ask for consent to them. There are no analytics, tracking or advertising cookies. When the open website is all you use, we don't set any cookies at all (Cloudflare may occasionally set a short-lived security cookie if it needs to check that a visitor isn't an automated attack).

Why we're allowed to use this information

  • To provide the service you asked for (teacher accounts, classes and subscriptions): this is necessary for our contract with you.
  • To keep the site secure and working (technical information and logs): our legitimate interests in running a safe, reliable website.
  • To keep financial records: a legal obligation (UK tax law).

Who we share it with

We only share information with the companies that help us run the site, and only for that purpose:

  • Cloudflare: hosting, and storing account and class information;
  • Google or Microsoft: only if you choose to sign in with them;
  • Stripe: payments.

Some of these companies may process information outside the UK, for example in the United States. Where they do, it's protected by safeguards recognised under UK data protection law (such as the UK–US "data bridge" or the UK's approved contract terms).

We'd only share information with anyone else if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Teacher accounts and classes: while your account is in use. We delete accounts that haven't been used for [2 years], or sooner if you ask.
  • Sign-in sessions: up to 30 days.
  • Payment and invoice records: 6 years, as UK tax law requires.
  • Technical logs: a few days.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop using it, and you can ask for a copy to take elsewhere. Email [EMAIL] and we'll reply within one month. You can cancel a subscription at any time from the Teachers page (Manage billing).

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

Children

The games are made for school students. We've designed them so that students don't need to share any personal information: no accounts, no names, no chat, no adverts and no tracking. Accounts are for teachers only.

Changes to this notice

If we change how we use information, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If a change affects teachers' accounts significantly, we'll tell them by email.
